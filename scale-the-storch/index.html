How to Score

✅ Every correct answer earns 10 points. 🔥 Get 3 correct answers in a row to unlock a Bonus Scale Roll. 🎲 The Bonus Scale Factor can be:
  • ½
  • 1
  • 1½
  • 2
Your points for that turn are multiplied by the Bonus Scale Factor. Example:
  • Correct Answer = 10 points
  • Bonus Roll = 1½
  • Total for that round = 15 points
Every time you reach a new streak of 3, 6, or 9 correct answers in a row, you earn another Bonus Scale Roll!
